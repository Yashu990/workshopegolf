Young Drive
At Paradise Junior Golf, we’re passionate about inspiring and developing the next generation of golfers.

 


Our Mission
Founded with a simple mission — to grow the game by making it fun, competitive, friendly, and affordable — our focus has always been on giving juniors opportunities to play and compete without the burden of high entry fees.


Leadership Development
We’re committed to helping young athletes build confidence, character, and leadership both on and off the course. Through our tournaments and programs, we emphasize teamwork, sportsmanship, and personal growth, creating an environment where every junior can thrive
